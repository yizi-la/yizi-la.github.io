Why we made it

Made for a desert morning.

We wanted the part of breakfast that feels like a ritual without the part that needs a blender, a sink full of parts, and twenty minutes you don't have.

So we took it down to three things that already work together — a seed that thickens, a sugar that comes from the valley an hour east of us, and fruit with the water taken out. Nothing else needed adding.

What's in the sachet

Five things. That's the whole list.

  • Chia seed Does the thickening. Drinks up the water and turns the whole thing into a base.
  • Date sugar Whole dates, dried and milled, from growers in the Coachella Valley.
  • Strawberry powder Dehydrated strawberries, milled fine. The colour is just the fruit.
  • Collagen peptides Unflavoured collagen that disappears into the mix. Adds protein without changing the taste.
  • Creatine monohydrate The most studied form of creatine. Pure, unflavored, and easy to take every day.

No gums, no fillers, no flavouring. Add half a cup of water and that's the recipe.

Coachella Valley, California

The sugar in your sachet grew ninety minutes from the pool it's sitting next to.

Date palms have been in this valley for over a century. We buy whole fruit from growers there, dry it, and mill it — so the sweetness arrives with the fibre and minerals still attached to it, instead of stripped out.

Ways to use it

One base. However your morning is going.

  • i As a bowl

    Let it sit the full three minutes, pour into a bowl, top with whatever's in the fridge.

  • ii As a smoothie

    Use a full cup of water instead of half, or blend it with a frozen banana.

  • iii Overnight

    Mix it into a jar at night, lid on, fridge. Thicker and ready before you are.

  • iv On top of things

    Thin it out and it works as a spoonable layer over yoghurt, oats or ice.

Questions

The things people ask.

Does it really take three minutes?

About twenty seconds of that is you. The rest is the chia absorbing the water and thickening — you can walk away for it. Leave it longer and it gets thicker.

Do I have to use the wand?

No. A spoon and thirty seconds of stirring gets you there. The wand just gets the powder fully dispersed faster, which is why it's in the starter pack.

Can I use milk instead of water?

Yes — dairy, oat, almond, all fine. Use the same half cup. It comes out richer and takes slightly longer to set.

What's actually in it?

Chia seed, date sugar and dehydrated strawberry. Nothing else — no gums, no preservatives, no added flavour or colour.
